04 — Research

Research with measurable impact.

Computer vision research connecting player positioning, court mapping and shuttlecock trajectory analysis in one badminton analytics framework.

6 pages Open paper ↗
Accepted by IEEE

Conference paper · Computer vision · Sports analytics

TrackVision: Identification and Analysis of Player Positions and Shuttlecock Trajectory Using YOLO and TrackNet Models

An integrated deep-learning framework that turns monocular badminton broadcast footage into structured tactical analytics. The system combines ResNet-50 court localization, YOLOv8 player detection, TrackNetV2 shuttlecock tracking, homography mapping and trajectory smoothing.
